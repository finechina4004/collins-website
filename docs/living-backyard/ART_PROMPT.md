## Final generation prompt

EDIT IMAGE 1, the user's chosen favorite Collins backyard illustration. Preserve this precise composition and scale: brick fireplace at left, two-story cream poolhouse in middle, outdoor kitchen under cedar pergola right, foreground cutaway pool with steps and ladder. Do NOT replace it with grander buildings, towers, a mansion, hotel or extra floors. Improve this chosen image into a much more authored, memorable, beautifully crafted illustrated website world. MATCH THE EXACT FLAT PAINTED DRAWING STYLE OF IMAGE 2 (the user's website reference): angular architectural silhouettes, subtle uneven gouache grain over crisp solid painted shapes, fine ironwork, flat directional shadows, little elongated eccentric human characters. Avoid generic digital storybook shading; the reference is flatter and sharper. Keep peach sky, brick orange, warm cream, sage and blue-green water but strengthen separation and warmth of colors. Refine poolhouse: sage louvered shutters open at the upper windows, an arched glass transom over the double doors, very fine cream cornice and one small unlettered oval sign above doors, lamp glowing warmly inside one upstairs window. Fireplace has a lively but neatly drawn flame, meticulously drawn irregular bricks, small tidy log stack beside hearth; the reader in ochre chair wears a pale blue shirt, long cream trousers and white socks, utterly serious expression reading an upside-down book. Keep empty other chair with a casually tossed coral towel. At kitchen: a cook with dark moustache and plain cream apron carefully inspects ONE lemon, companion in brick red holds a small bowl, two glasses and a striped cloth on table; maintain understated tiny-figure proportions. Preserve dog looking at a yellow tennis ball beside pool, add just two forgotten coral sandals on coping right. In the WATER, introduce the signature Collins toy submarine in IMAGE 3, REDRAWN IN THE SAME FLAT ILLUSTRATED STYLE, small at x53%, y77%, taking only about 15% of image width: mustard hull, cream oval front window rim, teal glass, coral fins, jointed cream periscope with red tip and tiny 01. No other submarine. Integrate softly with flat translucent blue-green wash and a few little painted bubbles, beautiful understated ripple and caustic pattern so the swimming pool reads as filled with WATER, not an empty dry pool. Keep ample quiet underwater space around submarine for navigation. Strong ordered composition and precise little material details, atmospheric but strictly 2D. No captions, labels, UI, watermarks or other lettering. No film characters. A full-bleed richly authored original Collins scene, in the actual website reference's drawing language. This is a refinement of Image 1, not a new composition.

## Review note

A prompt's requested details are not evidence that every detail appeared correctly. In particular, character expression, the book joke, exact vessel position and material treatment require visual acceptance. The newest owner instruction favors painterly storybook surfaces and softly shaded forms; it supersedes any overemphasis on flatness in this generation prompt. See ../VISUAL_STANDARD.md before further illustration work.
