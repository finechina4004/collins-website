# Pool artwork — integrated in the review branch

Created during the owner-authorized pool continuation and preserved immediately when the owner requested a workspace transfer. These are original decorative illustrations, never real construction evidence. The built-in image-generation tool produced them; WebP exports preserve each composition and alpha where present. No authentic project photographs were edited.

Source style: `public/assets/collins-illustrated-backyard.webp`, the working refinement of the owner's favorite. The new plates use the same cream poolhouse, left brick fireplace, right coral-striped pergola/kitchen, white/brown dog and muted turquoise tiled pool. User's locked language is painterly vintage storybook architectural cutaway, with gouache grain and softly shaded physical forms, not photorealistic miniature rendering or flat clipart.

- **Day plate:** edit only the baked-in submarine/bubbles out of the existing composition; paint matching empty water so a separate vessel can animate. Preserve composition, buildings, people, objects and palette.
- **Evening plate:** edit that clean plate into blue hour while preserving geometry. Dusty blue/teal sky, deep green foliage, warm mustard windows/door/lamps/fire/pergola lights, golden reflections and readable deep turquoise water. No vessel on plate.
- **Pool panorama:** closer front-facing 3:1 view of the same filled pool. Solid tiled steps at far left, clean continuous pool shell, open center, flush brass light at right-center, ladder far right, coved tiled floor, thin view of same cream/brick/pergola yard above. Restrained caustics, material grain and water haze. No vessel/dog/phone; no ocean, fish, underwater timber, shelves, brackets, pipes or caves.
- **Dog:** isolate the same white/brown dog from the homepage in a rightward leap. Brown head/floppy ears/back patches; white body/snouth/tail. Four legs visible. Painted texture, natural simplified proportions and genuine transparent silhouette. No ball/water/scene baked in.
- **Vessel:** isolate/redraw the same mustard submarine, cream hatch and jointed periscope with coral tip, coral rear fins, left brass propeller, small side porthole, large cream-rimmed teal front window on right, tiny 01. Soft gouache shading and grain. No halo, water, bubbles or background outside alpha.

The PNG masters were returned as generated images in this conversation. The five optimized WebPs are the portable canonical checkpoint assets. The previously generated photorealistic backyard proposals are rejected studies and are not included in the runtime asset set. The earlier alternate flat vessel sprite is not the selected final asset.

The five supplied exports are now integrated into the review branch and browser-checked in the pool journey. The original generation notes above are preserved as provenance.
