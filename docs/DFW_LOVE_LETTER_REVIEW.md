# Collins — For the place we call home

## Audit and design decisions
The live home is a simple photo and contact page without navigation. Existing About, Services, Work, Contact and eight project albums use a different visual system. The prior vintage prototype was not deployed and did not contain all approved images. This revision uses the actual two-font audit commit, exact modern home image, one global navigation and footer, restrained documentary captions, and a short editorial chapter introduction. It preserves existing project photography, gallery controls and URLs. New pages are Pool Design, Process, Portfolio and Areas We Serve; /work stays accessible for old links.

## Final photo selection
Home: existing modern Dallas backyard, no vintage treatment. About: Magnolia Pegasus at sunset. Services: full historic Arlington Stadium aerial. Pool Design: original 1961 Texas Pool aerial. Process: original uploaded Big Tex. Portfolio: vintage Southfork entrance postcard. Areas We Serve: uploaded black-and-white Six Flags entrance.

The Big Tex and Six Flags WebP files are present. Four exact archival originals are pending; do not replace them with generated history. Rights for the newly selected originals must be confirmed. Pending photos display labeled review placeholders, and new chapter pages are noindex. Both deploy and deployment check reject missing originals. This branch is not production-ready; keep main and Cloudflare unchanged until the files and rights are confirmed, then build and visually QA. The local preview is a separate interactive review of the direction. No font binaries changed.

## Editorial structure
Home: modern luxury. About: roots and family. Services: time together. Design: personal space. Process: people and craftsmanship. Portfolio: real projects. Service area: the next generation. No fictitious customer stories or claims that Collins built historical landmarks.
