# Underwater story revision

Owner feedback: the submerged pool looked fake, with an implausible ledge above the steps. Asked for more imaginative events including a dog jumping in and someone dropping a phone. This revision replaces the submerged set, removes the shelf/support architecture, preserves the physical Collins vessel and adds two independent animated stories. Original pools/94 photograph assets are unchanged.

## Art and construction

Original illustrative pool shell: assets/pool-shell-v2.webp, generated at 2172×724 with the established pool-day artwork as palette/set reference. Solid plaster entry steps descend into a continuous pool shell; smooth pale-blue plaster, a waterline tile band and coved floor replace underwater shelf brackets and exposed pipework. Render with cover / auto 100% to preserve the image aspect ratio; never stretch a 3:1 plate into a 5:1 or 6:1 view. The physical vessel remains a separate existing layer.

Original transparent dog: assets/dog-dive.webp, 1536×1024 RGBA source transcoded to alpha-preserving WebP. Golden Labrador-style miniature, coral collar, side-on leap. It is a fictional scene dog, not a claimed likeness of Colt or a real project photograph. Built-in image generation was used for both raster assets; no real project photo was edited. The phone, ball, splash/rings/bubbles and search light are original code-native props.

Prompt set: pool — same sage-fence/coral-awning/cream-coping backyard viewed underwater; realistic solid plaster steps only at extreme left, uninterrupted blue plaster wall and coved floor, genuine pool light and ladder at right, warm caustics, quiet empty swim space, no vessel/dog/phone/text, no floating shelf, timber, brackets, underwater pipes, arches or caves. Dog — one complete golden Labrador-style miniature on transparent alpha, four paws/tail/ears visible in horizontal rightward leap, natural wet fur and coral collar, warm upper-left light with aqua fill, no environment or lettering. Generated original files remain in the conversation image outputs; runtime copies are in public/assets.

## Experience

Five chapters: The Shallow End; An Unscheduled Arrival; The Unexpected Dive; The Evening Shift; Back at Poolside. Dog and phone run once on entering their chapters, with explicit replay buttons. Dog event: ball, leap, plume/rings/bubbles and a small vessel reaction, then a quiet dog pose near the surface. Phone event: rotating descent, bubbles and a submarine search light, then a readable still composition. Evening/periscope continue into the same poolside site.

No required collection, steering or hover. Motion-off/device reduced-motion use still poses and narrative status, preserving replay/control access. Animations and timers cancel on chapter/route departure or hidden tab. Keyboard focus snaps to its correct chapter to prevent focus-induced camera displacement; pointer focus avoids moving the camera during a click. Existing normal document scrolling remains.
